## Alternative segmentation approaches

I also searched for alternative segmentation approaches:

1. **Omnipose:** Particularly useful for irregular cell shapes and segmentation of touching objects.
2. **StarDist:** An instance-segmentation approach that can be useful for separating densely packed objects.

### Omnipose

This approach was developed to improve the segmentation of cells with difficult and irregular shapes.

It builds on ideas from Cellpose but changes how the model represents cells. In particular, it uses distance fields and their gradients to help reconstruct cell shapes.

The model reports improvements over Cellpose on its bacterial datasets.

I have already observed some issues in my own pipeline:

- Cellpose sometimes merges two neighbouring cells.
- Some predicted masks extend beyond the ground-truth boundaries.
- My evaluation does not yet distinguish individual cell errors.
- The model currently processes each frame independently.

Omnipose may provide an alternative way of representing cell shapes, especially when dealing with difficult or irregular shapes.

However, its strongest published results are on bacterial datasets, so this does not necessarily mean that it will perform better than Cellpose on my dataset.

I would first implement the evaluation methods I mentioned, especially those designed for instance segmentation. This would allow me to evaluate the current Cellpose results more accurately before comparing them with alternative segmentation models.
